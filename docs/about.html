What the maps are

Each map shows the research groups of one field in Europe: the EU, the UK, Switzerland and Norway.

One entry is one group: a PI, an institution and the group's homepage.

Members appear by name and role under their group; those two facts are all the map shows about them.

Groups are listed by institution, then by PI name.

  • multimodal AI · 466 groups · as of 2026-10-15

Data sources

Public academic pages: lab, group and institutional pages, read page by page; a sample is checked by hand.

Public funding registers, publication metadata, and award and competition pages.

These are the only sources.

Each group shows the date its page was last read.

Method

A group forms around its PI: a professor, a group leader named as such on a public page, or the lead of a grant.

A member joins a group when a public team page lists them with the PI, or when they work on the PI's grant at the same institution. People who only share papers with the PI are counted, not named. A team page that still lists someone whom a newer page places elsewhere does not count.

The institution is the one the PI's own page names. Spellings of one institution are joined through a public table of names. A second post elsewhere, such as an honorary professorship, is listed next to it, not in its place.

A card that says "members not yet collected" has no member linked from a public page yet. It does not mean the group has no members.

Before each publication four checks run. (a) At least 90% of a list of well-known groups in the field are on the map, with the same PI at the same institution. (b) At most 10% of groups were last read more than 12 months before the map's date. (c) The data is free of LinkedIn keys and URLs. (d) A random sample of groups is checked by hand against their pages. The current values:

Corrections and removal

To correct an entry, use "Report a correction" on the group's card, or the correction form. Say what is wrong and link a public page that shows the correct fact.

To be removed, use the removal form. Give your name as shown and the group, and say whether to remove you or the whole group. No reason is needed. The removal is made at the next publication.

Both forms are GitHub issues: they need a GitHub account and are public.

For each person the map stores the name, role, institution, group, the public URLs these come from, and the date the page was read. Nothing else.

地图是什么

每张地图展示一个领域在欧洲（欧盟、英国、瑞士和挪威）的研究课题组。

一个条目就是一个课题组：负责人、机构和课题组主页。

成员以姓名和角色列在所属课题组下，地图展示的只有这两项。

课题组按机构、再按负责人姓名排列。

  • multimodal AI · 466 个课题组 · 截至 2026-10-15

数据来源

学术公开页面：实验室、课题组和机构页面，逐页阅读，抽样人工核查。

公开的资助登记、论文元数据，以及奖项和竞赛页面。

来源仅限于此。

每个课题组都标明其页面最近一次阅读的日期。

方法

课题组以负责人为中心：教授、公开页面上写明的课题组长，或资助项目的负责人。

公开的团队页面把某人与负责人列在一起，或某人在同一机构参与负责人的资助项目，此人即为成员。只与负责人合写过论文的人只计数，不列名。如果较新的页面显示某人已在别处，仍列着此人的旧团队页面不算数。

机构取负责人本人页面上的机构。同一机构的不同写法通过公开的名称表合并。在别处的第二职位（如名誉教授）列在旁边，不取代主机构。

卡片上写"成员未采集"，表示还没有从公开页面关联到成员，并不表示该课题组没有成员。

每次发布前运行四项检查：(a) 该领域知名课题组清单中至少 90% 在图上，负责人和机构一致；(b) 最近一次阅读早于地图日期 12 个月以上的课题组不超过 10%；(c) 数据中领英字段和链接的计数为 0；(d) 随机抽取若干课题组，人工对照其页面核查。当前数值：

更正与删除

要更正条目，请点课题组卡片上的"报告错误"，或使用更正表单：说明哪里有误，并附上能证明正确信息的公开页面。

要求删除，请使用删除表单：填写页面上显示的姓名和课题组，并选择删除本人还是整个课题组。无需说明理由。删除在下一次发布时生效。

两个表单都是 GitHub issue：需要 GitHub 账号，内容公开。

对每个人，地图只存储姓名、角色、机构、课题组、这些信息所来自的公开链接，以及页面阅读日期。
