# EuroSciVoc paths commonly used to filter CORDIS

CORDIS tags projects with the European Science Vocabulary. Filter the `euroSciVocPath` column
(name varies by release) by substring. Starting points:

- `/natural sciences/computer and information sciences` (AI, software, HPC)
- `/engineering and technology/electrical engineering, electronic engineering, information engineering` (electronics, semiconductors, robotics, telecommunications)
- `/engineering and technology/mechanical engineering` (robotics mechanics, automation)
- `/engineering and technology/nanotechnology` (devices, materials)

Combine with free-text keyword matching on title + objective; EuroSciVoc tagging is coarse.
